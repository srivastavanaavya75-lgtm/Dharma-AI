# ⚖️ Dharma AI

### Legal Intelligence Workspace

Dharma AI is a modern legal intelligence platform designed to bring legal research, document analysis, AI-assisted drafting, compliance analysis, constitutional research, and lawyer consultations into one unified workspace.

> **Dharma AI v4.4**

---

## ✨ Overview

Dharma AI combines a professional legal workspace with AI-powered tools to help users research laws, analyze legal documents, explore judgments, understand constitutional provisions, and connect with legal professionals.

The platform is designed around a simple idea:

**Research → Analyze → Draft → Consult**

---

## 🚀 Features

### 🤖 AI Legal Agents

Dharma AI provides specialized AI agents for different legal workflows:

- **Research Agent**  
  Research laws, sections, judgments, and legal principles.

- **Drafting Agent**  
  Generate structured legal drafts and notices.

- **Compliance Agent**  
  Identify applicable regulations, compliance requirements, and legal risks.

- **Constitution Agent**  
  Analyze constitutional provisions, doctrines, and landmark judgments.

---

### 🔎 Legal Research

Search across a structured legal knowledge interface covering:

- Constitution
- Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS)
- Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS)
- Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA)
- Central Acts
- Rules
- Circulars
- Notifications
- Judgments

Research results provide:

- Relevant legal provisions
- Cross references
- Landmark judgments
- Ask AI actions
- Bookmarking
- Citation copying

---

### 📄 Document Intelligence

Upload legal documents and receive structured analysis including:

- Executive summary
- Parties
- Timeline
- Applicable laws
- Legal risks
- Recommendations
- AI-generated insights

Documents can also be exported for further use.

---

### 👨‍⚖️ Lawyer Network

Browse verified legal professionals by:

- Specialisation
- Location
- Experience
- Rating
- Availability

Supported practice areas include:

- Criminal Law
- Civil Law
- Corporate Law
- Family Law
- Property Law
- Tax Law
- Intellectual Property
- Labour Law
- Cyber Law
- Constitutional Law

---

### 📅 Consultations

Book consultations with lawyers directly through the platform.

Users can:

- Select a preferred date and time
- Describe their legal matter
- Submit consultation requests
- Confirm appointments
- Reschedule consultations
- Cancel consultations
- View consultation details
- Track pending, upcoming, completed, and cancelled sessions

---

- Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam
