What is the punishment for cheating under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita?
Legal analysis
BNS replaced Section 420 of the erstwhile IPC. It retains the core test — deception causing wrongful loss or gain — but restructures cheating into graded sub-clauses based on whether property, a valuable security, or a position of trust is involved.

Section 318 — Cheating
Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 › Chapter XVII › Property Offences
BNS
Defines cheating as deceiving a person to induce them to deliver property, consent to retention of property, or do/omit an act they would not otherwise do — with graded punishment based on the nature of the property or trust involved.
Cross referencesAsk AIBookmarkCopy citation
Ram Narain v. State of U.P.
Allahabad High Court › Criminal Appeal › Cheating
Judgment
Held that dishonest intention must exist at the time of the promise for an offence of cheating to be made out; a subsequent breach of a genuine promise is a civil wrong, not a criminal one.

Article 21 — Protection of life and personal liberty
Constitution of India › Part III › Fundamental Rights
Constitution
No person shall be deprived of their life or personal liberty except according to procedure established by law — the foundation for due-process protections read into every criminal statute.
